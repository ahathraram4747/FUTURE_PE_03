# SEO Prompt Output Samples

## Sample 1 - Keyword and Search Intent

### Prompt

Identify the primary keyword, secondary keywords, and search intent for a local business offering digital marketing services in Chennai.

### Output

Primary Keyword:
digital marketing agency in Chennai

Secondary Keywords:
- digital marketing services in Chennai
- SEO services in Chennai
- social media marketing Chennai
- local SEO services Chennai
- online marketing agency Chennai

Search Intent:
Commercial investigation. The user is likely comparing digital marketing services and looking for an agency that can help improve their online visibility.

## Sample 3 - Supporting Content Cluster

### Prompt

Create four supporting blog topics around the pillar topic "Digital Marketing Agency in Chennai."

### Output

1. SEO Services in Chennai: A Beginner's Guide for Small Businesses
2. Local SEO in Chennai: How Local Businesses Can Improve Online Visibility
3. Social Media Marketing in Chennai: A Practical Guide for Small Businesses
4. How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in Chennai

Each supporting article is connected to the pillar article through relevant internal links.

## Sample 4 - Local SEO

### Prompt

Suggest natural local SEO keywords for a digital marketing business targeting customers in Chennai.

### Output

- digital marketing agency in Chennai
- SEO services in Chennai
- local SEO services Chennai
- social media marketing Chennai
- digital marketing services in Chennai

The keywords should be used naturally and should not be repeated excessively.

---

## Prompt Engineering Approach

The prompts use structured instructions, keyword context, search intent, target location, heading requirements, internal linking requirements, and content quality guidelines.

This approach helps generate consistent SEO content while keeping the writing useful and readable for real users.
